### 🎨 DESIGN REQUIREMENTS

**Theme:** Pure white (#FFFFFF) background with soft off-white sections (#F8F9FA). NO dark backgrounds anywhere.

**Color Palette:**
- Primary: #25D366 (WhatsApp green) for CTAs and accents
- Secondary: #128C7E (deep WhatsApp teal) for hover states
- Text: #1A1A2E (deep navy) for headings, #4A4A68 for body text
- Accent glows: rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.15) for subtle green glow effects
- Border: rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.2) for card borders

**Typography:**
- Heading font: `'Clash Display'` or `'Plus Jakarta Sans'` from Google Fonts (bold, modern)
- Body font: `'DM Sans'` from Google Fonts (clean, readable)
- Hero heading: 72px, font-weight 800
- Sub-headings: 40px, font-weight 700
- Body: 16px, line-height 1.7
